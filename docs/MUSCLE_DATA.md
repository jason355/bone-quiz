## Lab.txt discrepancies & decisions / Lab.txt 差異與處理

- **Vastus intermedialis** (Lab.txt) → displayed as the standard *Vastus intermedius*; the Lab spelling is accepted as an alias.
- **伸姆趾長肌** (Lab.txt) → standard 伸拇趾長肌 (extensor hallucis longus); the Lab characters are accepted as an alias.
- **Calcaneal (Achilies) tendon** — “Achilies” is a typo of *Achilles*; both spellings accepted.
- **腓長肌 / 腓短肌** (Lab.txt) are the same as the standard 腓骨長肌 / 腓骨短肌; both accepted.
- **Brachioradialis** is listed by Lab.txt under the forearm *flexor* superficial layer, but anatomically it lies in the *extensor (posterior)* compartment and is supplied by the **radial nerve**. The quiz keeps Lab.txt’s placement for the “compartment” question and states the radial nerve for the nerve question. **Please confirm which your lecturer wants.**
- **Deltoid** has its own region (三角肌區) because Lab.txt lists it alone under “Arm”. **Triceps** is posterior compartment of arm as in Lab.txt.
- **Sternocleidomastoid** is written without a period in Lab.txt; treated as a neck muscle.
- **Erector spinae, quadriceps femoris, thenar, hypothenar, calcaneal tendon** are groups/tendon: they are one entry each (their 3D model is the group).
- **Z-Anatomy origin/insertion markers** were reversed for trapezius, pectoralis minor, serratus anterior and rectus abdominis; they were re-classified by the nearest bone so blue = origin and green = insertion are anatomically correct. (Rectus abdominis should be re-checked by eye.)
- Cranial nerves and CNS structures in Lab.txt belong to the next module (nerves / neuro) and are **not** in this muscle bank.
